📞 Inbound Phone Process
  • Answer within 3 rings. "Thank you for calling Pat Lobb Toyota McKinney, this is [Name], how can I help you?"
  • Get their name and use it immediately. Build rapport in the first 30 seconds.
  • Identify the vehicle they're calling about. Ask about their current vehicle.
  • DO NOT quote price on the phone. "The best way to get you an accurate number is to come in — can we set that up?"
  • Set the appointment. Offer two time options. "I have today at 3pm or tomorrow at 11am — which works better for you?"
  • Get their cell number and email. Confirm the appointment time.
  • Send Covideo immediately after hanging up.
📞 Outbound Call Process
  • Identify yourself and the dealership. Keep your energy HIGH.
  • Reference WHY you're calling. "I'm reaching out because we have a [vehicle] that matches exactly what you were looking for..."
  • If voicemail — leave a SHORT message (under 20 seconds) and send a Covideo immediately after.
  • Attempt 7 contacts before marking as unable to reach. Mix calls, texts, and Covideos.
  • Goal every call: SET THE APPOINTMENT.
Pro tip: First one to speak after asking for the appointment loses. Ask — then stay silent.
👋 Meet & Greet Process
  • Greet within 60 seconds of arrival. Smile. Make eye contact. Firm handshake.
  • "Welcome to Pat Lobb Toyota McKinney! I'm [Name] — are you here to see anyone specific or can I help you out today?"
  • Get their name and use it. "Great to meet you [Name]!"
  • Find out what brings them in today. Listen MORE than you talk.
  • If appointment — confirm who they're seeing and notify that SP immediately.
  • Log the customer into the Greeter system before leaving the welcome area.
  • Walk them to the vehicle or desk — never point.
💬 Key Discovery Questions
  • What brings you in today?
  • What are you currently driving?
  • Are you looking to trade that in?
  • Do you have a specific vehicle in mind?
  • Is this primarily for you or someone else?
  • What's most important to you in your next vehicle?
  • Have you been to Pat Lobb before?
🚗 Customer Vehicle Evaluation

The CVE is completed on EVERY customer with a trade-in. This is your ammunition for the desk.

  • Step 1 — Vehicle ID: Get the VIN or enter year/make/model. Ask for mileage verbally — customer states it out loud.
  • Step 2 — Q&A: 6 questions. How long owned? Accidents? Mechanical issues? Loan? Two keys? Upgrades?
  • Step 3 — Walk-Around: 13-point inspection ON SITE. Or phone notes for Site Unseen.
  • Step 4 — PP Hooks: Toggle on each protection product present. Read the script. Record YES/SOMEWHAT/INDIFFERENT.
  • Step 5 — Summary & Sign-Off: Review with customer. Type your name. Submit to Firebase.
Remember: The CVE summary goes directly to the UCM. PP temperature is visible before you even get to the desk.
🎯 Soft Trade — "If It Makes Sense"
"What would 'make sense' look like for you on the trade-in?"

Enter their number and the UCM rough range. The gap calculator will show:

  • 🟢 Under $1,000 gap — WORKABLE, close today
  • 🟡 $1,000-$3,000 gap — CLOSEABLE, tax savings play
  • 🔴 Over $3,000 gap — RESET, get vehicle in first
"If we could get you to [UCM Range] — would you be ready to move forward today?"

🎬 Covideo — The Right Way

Covideo is sent immediately after hanging up the phone. No delay — strike while the iron is hot.

If daylight / good lighting
Go to the vehicle immediately. Record ONE video covering both face/name intro AND vehicle walkaround. Show key features. Keep it under 90 seconds.
"Hey [Name], it's [Your Name] from Pat Lobb Toyota McKinney — just wanted to put a face with a name and show you this incredible [vehicle] we have waiting for you! [walks around car] Can't wait to see you [day/time] — see you soon!"
If too dark / bad lighting
Send Face/Name video immediately. Promise the car video for next morning.
"Hey [Name], it's [Your Name] — leaving now but wanted to put a face with a name! I'll reach out with a video of the vehicle before you arrive to answer your questions and show off this terrific [vehicle] — see you soon!"
